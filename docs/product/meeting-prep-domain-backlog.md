# Meeting Prep Domain Backlog

FluentIT should promote domains when they improve technical meeting preparation, not just when they add more glossary coverage. A promoted domain should materially improve agenda and thread term extraction, role-specific prep packs, meeting-question generation, or spaced review after a meeting.

## First Focus Domains

These domains are now part of the content catalog because they appear in many cross-functional technical meetings and they expose useful trade-off language:

| Domain | Meeting Prep Value |
| --- | --- |
| Observability & SRE | Helps with incident reviews, launch readiness, reliability targets, and monitoring trade-offs. |
| Identity & Access | Helps with authentication, enterprise SSO, access review, and zero-trust discussions. |
| Architecture & System Design | Helps users follow design reviews, scaling debates, and failure-mode conversations. |
| Application Security & DevSecOps | Helps with threat reviews, secure delivery, vulnerability triage, and release risk. |

## Deferred Domains

| Domain | Why Later |
| --- | --- |
| IT Operations & Service Management | Useful for incident, problem, change, CMDB, and service-desk meetings after the meeting-prep loop is proven. |
| Storage, Backup & Disaster Recovery | Strong fit for resilience reviews, but partly overlaps current cloud and systems content. |
| Enterprise SaaS & Business Systems | Good for CRM, ERP, HRIS, procurement, and vendor-integration meetings once role tracks expand. |
| Workplace & Endpoint IT | Useful for MDM, EDR, patching, and device posture discussions for internal IT audiences. |
| FinOps & Vendor Management | Valuable for cloud cost, licensing, contract, and chargeback conversations when business-facing prep packs land. |
| AI Governance & AI Security | Important for model risk, prompt injection, evaluation, and privacy once AI meeting prep becomes a dedicated track. |

## Promotion Questions

Before promoting a deferred domain, answer:

1. Which meeting agendas or role tracks need it now?
2. Which concepts are missing from existing domains rather than merely duplicated?
3. Which prep packs and question prompts become better after the domain exists?
4. Can the domain support at least 50 reviewed cards with clear meeting trade-offs?
